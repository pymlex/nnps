# H02 — Elasticity versus formation-enthalpy Pareto

Status: proposed  
Track: B

## Claim

The feasible set of simultaneous errors in $C_{ij}$ and $\Delta H_f$ for Al–Ni is constrained inside pure EAM by Cauchy-pressure physics. Measured hypervolume of the Pareto front grows then saturates as residual capacity rises from pair to attention to full MLIP. Foundation checkpoints can be placed on the same plane.

## Method

Pareto hypernetwork or Chebyshev scalarisation over elastic-path losses and formation/defect losses. Classes: EAM, pair residual, three-body residual, attention residual, full MLIP.

## Data needs

Experiment and/or teacher MLIP. DFT optional.

## Decision

Backup if DFT is delayed. Can also be a chapter supporting H01.
